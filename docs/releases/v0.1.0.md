# UK Tax Tracker 0.1.0

The first release of UK Tax Tracker: a self-hosted app for working out UK capital gains, dividend and share-scheme tax from your own records. It runs on your own computer or server, and your data is encrypted on it.

## Install

**macOS and Linux**

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/apilbeam101/tax-tracker/releases/download/v0.1.0/install.sh | sh
```

**Windows** (PowerShell)

```powershell
& ([scriptblock]::Create((irm https://github.com/apilbeam101/tax-tracker/releases/download/v0.1.0/install.ps1)))
```

The installer sets up Node.js if it is missing (it never replaces one you have), installs the app, and can start it at login. Then open <http://127.0.0.1:3000> and create your account. Prefer to read the script first, or run it on a server? See the [installation guide](https://github.com/apilbeam101/tax-tracker/blob/v0.1.0/docs/INSTALL.md): Docker, Kubernetes, a systemd service, or `npx uk-tax-tracker`.

## What it does

### Capital gains
- Matches disposals the way HMRC requires: same day, then the following 30 days, then the Section 104 pool.
- Produces the computation HMRC asks for on each disposal, with totals that reconcile to the SA108 capital gains figures. Download it as a PDF report, or as plain text to paste into "Any other information".
- Applies the annual exempt amount and the CGT rates for each tax year, including the mid-year rate change in 2024-25. Rates for 2020-21 to 2026-27 are included, and they are data, not code.
- Handles splits, capital returns, rights issues, transfers and dividend reinvestment.

### Dividends
- Works out dividend tax with the allowance and your rate band, including foreign tax credit relief.
- Fills in US withholding (15% under the UK-US treaty) on USD dividends when you have not entered it. Your own figure always wins.
- Imports dividend history from Alpha Vantage.

### Employee share schemes
- Records RSU vests with any of the three withholding methods (net settlement, sell-to-cover, cash) and ESPP purchases including the discount.
- Projects upcoming vests and purchases, and matches each one to the real transaction when it arrives.

### Currencies and prices
- Converts to GBP with HMRC's monthly exchange rates (the default), daily spot rates, or a rate you enter. Every transaction keeps the rate, its type and its source.
- Live prices from Tiingo, falling back to Yahoo Finance.

### Your data
- One user, on your own machine. No account to create elsewhere, and the app contacts only the exchange-rate, price and dividend providers it needs.
- Sensitive fields are encrypted in the database file. They are unlocked by your passphrase, with a recovery key for when you forget it. By default the data is locked again after every restart.
- Always-on servers can opt in to unlocking by themselves, and the encryption key can be replaced at any time.

### Importing, exporting and backups
- Add transactions in the web app, through the REST API, or by pasting a CSV with a column mapping and a preview before anything is saved.
- Export transactions and disposals as CSV, in the cgtcalculator.com format, or as the annual report.
- Automatic daily backups, a backup before every upgrade, and restore from the app or the command line. Encrypted archives move your data between installs.

### Running it
- Installers for macOS, Windows and Linux; a Docker image for amd64 and arm64 with an optional HTTPS setup; Kubernetes manifests; a hardened systemd unit.
- `tax-tracker doctor` checks an installation and `tax-tracker backup` and `restore` manage your data from the command line.
- Releases carry build provenance. The image has a software bill of materials for each platform, and the npm package and installers can be verified against this repository.

## Good to know

- This is an early (0.x) release. Back up before upgrading (one is taken automatically) and read the release notes.
- **It is not tax advice.** It helps you work out your figures from your records; check them against HMRC's guidance, and ask a qualified adviser when in doubt.
