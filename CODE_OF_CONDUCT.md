# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want to get their UK share taxes right, and for the people who help
make that possible. Everyone taking part is expected to treat the others with respect, whatever
their background or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to tax. Questions are welcome.
- Assume good faith, and disagree about the work, not the person.
- Give feedback that helps: say what is wrong and, where you can, how to fix it.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other people's personal or financial information, and do not post
  your own real financial data either: this repository is public.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning language or behaviour.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Anything else that would reasonably make others feel unwelcome.

## Where this applies

In the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space run in its name, and when
someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock conversations, and block people who do
not follow this code, without notice and without having to explain each decision.

To report a conduct problem privately, use **Report a vulnerability** on this repository's
**Security** tab and say in the first line that it is a conduct report, not a security one. The
report is seen only by the maintainer, and its author is not named to anyone else.
